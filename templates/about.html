Target

One match, one winner

The model target is win = 1 for the match-winning team (WWCD) and 0 for the other teams in that lobby. BGIS uses the source file's existing win flag because it has no raw WWCD column; this assumption is documented and should be checked against the source scorecards.

The displayed probability is for one team's chance of winning one future match. It does not estimate the chance of winning the full BMSD tournament.

Feature engineering

Past results only

For every team-match row, historical averages, recent-three-match form, consistency, matches played, and pre-match ELO use only matches completed before the row's match. A team's current match points, kills, and outcome are added to its history only for later matches.

The 2026 BGIS fragment in the folder has only two teams per match, so it is excluded from complete-match training. Damage, survival time, assists, and player-level features are not present in the accepted data and are not invented.

Validation strategy

Chronological calibration

Model comparison uses the latest six complete matches as a forward holdout. Calibration windows expand through earlier match groups. All rows for a match stay together, so a match cannot be split between train and validation.

Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting are compared. The selected estimator minimizes conditional match log loss; Brier score is the tie-breaker. Class weighting and SMOTE are not used because they distort the natural one-winner-per-lobby probability rate.

Probability meaning

An estimate, not a guarantee

Predictions are calibrated statistical estimates from a small historical sample. Their reliability may change with rosters, format, stage, map pool, and the quality of newly imported scorecards. Model feature importance describes model behavior; it does not establish cause and effect.
